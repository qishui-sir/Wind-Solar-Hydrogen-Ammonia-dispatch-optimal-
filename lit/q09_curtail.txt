TITLE   : Developing effective market-based policies to incentivise fair and efficient European electricity transmission grid expansion
AUTHORS : Sam Vandezande; Richard Green; Raphael Slade
JOURNAL : 
YEAR    : 2026  | CITED: 0 | TYPE: posted-content
DOI     : https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6678966
ABSTRACT: European electricity transmission grids require unprecedented investment driven by growing electricity demand, ageing infrastructure and new load patterns from renewables and distributed generation. This large expansion has triggered National Regulatory Agencies (NRA) to review the policies that they set on Transmission System Operators (TSOs) remuneration and transmission tariffs which allocate the costs and benefits of investments between grid users and investors. Many alternative incentives and market-signal mechanisms have been introduced in recent years that aim to ensure that grids are developed and used efficiently. While these evolutions undoubtedly confer improvements, they also pose efficiency and fairness challenges. Drawing on in-depth interviews with NSO and TSO experts, we explore how policies can support efficient, fair and future-proof grid development. We find that whilst there is no optimal portfolio of TSO incentives, transmission tariffs and policies should provide users with time &amp; locational signals based on their abilities to respond. We also find a need for better alignment between transmission tariffs and TSO remuneration policies to unlock synergies in targeting expansion, security and decarbonisation objectives. Further evolution in transmission policies is required to balance efficiency and fairness. This evolution will require not only an increase in the granularity of policies with more targeted project incentives and user tariffs but also require increased collaboration between market players such as TSO, Distribution System Operators, energy retailers and grid users for the efficient development and use of the transmission grid.
